# 📁 HTML Portfolio – Organized Structure (Semantic)

## 1️⃣ Overall Page Structure

Every **proper HTML page** should mentally follow this order:

```
Header  → Identity
Nav     → Navigation
Main    → Actual content
Footer  → Ending info
```

---

## 2️⃣ Semantic Tag Organization (Clean & Normal)

### 🔹 `<header>` — Identity Section

**Purpose:**
Who you are

**Contains:**

* Your name
* Your role (CSE Student)
* Short intro line

**Rule:**
❌ Don’t put all content here
✔ Only intro information

---

### 🔹 `<nav>` — Navigation Section

**Purpose:**
Where users can go

**Contains:**

* Links to sections/pages

  * Summary
  * Education
  * Skills
  * Contact

**Why important:**
Screen readers recognize it as navigation

---

### 🔹 `<main>` — Main Content (ONLY ONE)

**Purpose:**
The real content of the page

**Contains:**

* All important sections
* No header, nav, or footer inside

---

## 3️⃣ Content Inside `<main>`

### 🔹 `<section>` — Topic Group

**Purpose:**
Group related content

**Examples:**

* Summary
* Skills
* Projects
* Activities

**Rule:**
✔ Every section must have a heading
❌ No heading = bad HTML

---

### 🔹 `<article>` — Independent Item

**Purpose:**
A single, reusable piece of content

**Examples:**

* One degree
* One project
* One blog post

**Rule:**
✔ Can stand alone
✔ Can exist inside a section

---

## 4️⃣ Contact Information (Important)

### 🔹 `<address>` — Contact Section

**Purpose:**
Contact details of the author

**Contains ONLY:**

* Email
* Phone
* GitHub
* Social links

**Should NOT contain:**

* Skills
* Education
* Random text

**Why it matters:**

* Accessibility
* SEO
* Professional HTML

---

## 5️⃣ `<footer>` — Ending Section

**Purpose:**
Wrap-up information

**Contains:**

* Copyright
* Author info
* Extra links (optional)

**Can be:**

* At page bottom
* At section bottom

---

## 6️⃣ Final Mental Model (Very Important)

```
<header>   → Who am I?
<nav>      → Where can you go?
<main>
   <section> → One topic
      <article> → One item
   </section>
   <section>
      <address> → Contact info
   </section>
</main>
<footer>   → End info
```
